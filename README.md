# 🚀 AI Market Intelligence Agent

A comprehensive market intelligence solution leveraging AI to analyze Brazilian e-commerce data and generate actionable business insights for market entry.

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on building an AI-assisted Market Research Agent that analyzes the **Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Dataset**. It demonstrates the ability to translate raw Kaggle datasets into executive-level decision guidance, complete with reliability scoring and visual evidence.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
* **AI/LLM:** Amazon QuickSuite (Market Intelligence Agent)
* **Data Source:** [Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce)
* **Focus Areas:** E-commerce Strategy, Market Analysis, Risk Assessment

## 📁 Repository Structure
* `data/`: Contains the raw Brazilian E-Commerce CSV files (orders, customers, products, etc.).
* `evidence/`: Screenshots of AI-assisted research and visual market signals.
* `research-brief.pdf`: The final executive summary and market entry recommendation.
* `market-analysis.md`: Detailed breakdown of Brazilian market opportunities and challenges.
* `reliability-evaluation.md`: Risk assessment and confidence scoring for each key insight.
* `quick-research-log.md`: Log of the specific queries and answers generated by the AI agent.

## 📈 Key Insights Discovered
1. **Market Growth:** Brazil's e-commerce is projected to grow significantly, representing a prime entry opportunity.
2. **Consumer Expectations:** Free shipping is a major driver of conversion; high cart abandonment occurs without it.
3. **Category Signals:** Beauty, Health, and Electronics are top-performing categories but face high local competition.
4. **Operational Risks:** Last-mile delivery outside major urban centers has high return and cancellation rates.
