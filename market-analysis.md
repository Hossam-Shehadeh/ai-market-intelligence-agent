# Market Analysis

## Internal dataset evidence
The Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Kaggle dataset contains 99,441 orders, 112,650 order items, and 32,951 products. The order history spans September 2016 to October 2018.

## Insight 1: The historical marketplace shows strong fulfillment completion
**Insight summary:** 96,478 of 99,441 orders were delivered, or 97.0%; 625 orders were canceled, or 0.6%.

**Supporting evidence/signals:** Olist order_status values from the Kaggle orders table.

**Why it matters:** A Brazil entry plan should protect the delivery experience because reliable fulfillment is a prerequisite for repeat purchase and category expansion.

**Recommended next step:** Pilot with logistics partners and monitor delivery completion, cancellation, late-delivery rate, and post-delivery review sentiment from day one.

## Insight 2: Category concentration supports a focused launch
**Insight summary:** The largest historical item-price totals in the prepared profile were beleza_saude (1,258,681.34), relogios_presentes (1,205,005.68), cama_mesa_banho (1,036,988.68), esporte_lazer (988,048.97), and informatica_acessorios (911,954.32).

**Supporting evidence/signals:** Item prices joined to product categories from the Kaggle item and product tables.

**Why it matters:** NovaTech should avoid a broad launch. A focused category portfolio can concentrate merchandising, seller acquisition, and customer-support readiness.

**Recommended next step:** Shortlist beauty/health, watches/gifts, and home categories for controlled tests, then validate margin and repeat behavior before scaling.

## Insight 3: The typical order is small and suggests basket-building potential
**Insight summary:** The dataset contains 112,650 order items across 99,441 orders, or approximately 1.14 items per order.

**Supporting evidence/signals:** Distinct order count and order-item count from the Kaggle tables.

**Why it matters:** A low item-per-order ratio may indicate single-item missions or limited cross-sell. It creates an opportunity, but the data alone does not prove why the basket is small.

**Recommended next step:** Test bundles, complementary recommendations, and free-shipping thresholds while measuring conversion, contribution margin, and repeat purchase.

## Insight 4: Historical evidence is useful for direction, not forecasting
**Insight summary:** The internal data ends in October 2018, while the market decision is current. Category and fulfillment patterns are useful baseline signals but cannot by themselves establish 2026 demand or competitive position.

**Supporting evidence/signals:** Dataset date range and the need for current external research.

**Why it matters:** Leadership should combine the historical marketplace pattern with fresh external signals before making an investment decision.

**Recommended next step:** Require a current competitor, pricing, logistics, and consumer-signal refresh before committing launch capital.

## Market conclusion
A constrained Brazil pilot is more defensible than a full-market launch. The strongest initial thesis is focused category testing with explicit delivery and basket-size experiments, subject to current external research and unit-economics validation.
