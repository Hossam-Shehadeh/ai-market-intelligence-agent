# Research Brief

## Research Objective
Should NovaTech enter the Brazilian e-commerce market, and which product categories and operating capabilities should be prioritized during a controlled first phase?

## Decision supported
Leadership needs a go/no-go recommendation, an initial category focus, and practical safeguards for delivery and customer experience.

## Scope of Research
### Included
- Brazilian e-commerce category demand signals
- Product and order patterns in the Kaggle Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset
- Delivery, cancellation, and customer-experience considerations
- Competitor and market signals from current external research
- First-phase recommendations and measurable risks

### Excluded
- A full TAM/SAM/SOM forecast
- Legal, tax, and country-entry implementation advice
- A causal claim that the historical dataset predicts future Brazilian demand
- Customer-level personally identifiable analysis

## Constraints
- Internal dataset reflects historical marketplace activity from September 2016 through October 2018.
- External signals should be captured with source name, publication date, and URL.
- Recommendations must be directional rather than presented as a financial forecast.
- Research effort should prioritize decision-changing signals over broad industry description.

## Research Approach
1. Profile the Kaggle dataset to establish category, order, delivery, and cancellation signals.
2. Use Quick Research to ask focused questions about current Brazilian e-commerce growth, consumer expectations, logistics, and competitive dynamics.
3. Compare internal historical signals with external market signals.
4. Separate observed facts from interpretation and recommendation.
5. Score each insight for source quality, consistency, timeliness, and overall confidence.

## Visual Evidence
The research utilizes a horizontal bar chart ([`evidence/top-category-demand.png`](evidence/top-category-demand.png)) that compares historical item-price totals by category. This visualization directly supports the category-selection insight by demonstrating the high revenue concentration in the top five categories (e.g., beleza_saude, relogios_presentes), reinforcing the recommendation for a focused market entry pilot rather than a broad launch.
